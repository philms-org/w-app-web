**Subject:** You're in! Meet your fellow builders at #TSWWS26 with The W App

Hi {{first_name}},

Welcome to **Techstars Startup Weekend: AI: Ideas in Action** at **Sparq – Innovation Quarter**, Winston-Salem, **Oct. 2–4, 2026**. We can't wait to see what you build.

This weekend we're using **The W App** as the event's live room. It lets you see who's here, find co-founders and teammates, get announcements from the organizers, and keep every connection you make after Sunday's pitches.

### Get set up in 2 minutes (do it before Friday)

1. **Open the app on your phone:** https://w-app-web.vercel.app
   There's nothing to download. Tip: tap *Share → Add to Home Screen* (iPhone) or *⋮ → Add to Home screen* (Android) so it opens like an app.
2. **Create your account.** Tap **Get started** and enter your name, email and password, then add a profile photo so teammates can recognise you in the room.
3. **Fill in your profile.** Add your role (developer, designer, business, domain expert…), what you're working on, and what you're looking for. This is how people find a team.
4. **Allow location when asked.** The app uses it *only* to check you in at the venue. See the privacy note below.

### When you arrive at Sparq

- **Check in.** Open the app inside the building and tap the **“You’re here — check in”** banner for Sparq (or **Check In Here** on the Map tab). You have to be on site; check-in works within the venue's geofence.
- **See who's here.** Your Home screen becomes the event room, showing everyone checked in with their role and what they're looking for.
- **Post to the room.** Share your idea, ask for a teammate ("Need a front-end dev for an AI tutoring idea!"), or just say hi. Like and comment on other posts.
- **Watch the announcement bar.** Organizers pin schedule updates, mentor office hours, and judging reminders at the top of the room.
- **Connect with people.** Met someone good? Open **Connections → Scan a code** and scan their QR code, or show yours. Connections stay in your app after the weekend.
- **Message privately.** Use **Messages** for 1:1 chats with teammates, mentors and fellow attendees. The venue chat is open to everyone checked in.

### Tips for the weekend

- **Friday:** after pitches, post your idea and the skills you need, then use *Who's here* to recruit.
- **Saturday:** watch the announcement bar for mentor availability and schedule changes.
- **Sunday:** connect with your team, mentors and judges before you leave. Those connections are yours to keep.
- See something inappropriate? Tap the **flag icon** on any post or comment and an organizer will review it.

### Privacy, in plain English

- Your location is used to check you in and show you in the room. Raw location data is deleted within 48 hours.
- Your email and phone number are **never** shown to other attendees.
- You control which profile details are visible in **Profile** settings. Full policy: https://w-app-web.vercel.app/privacy
