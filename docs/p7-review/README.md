# P7 dark-glass review (founder)

Published review page: https://claude.ai/artifact/LxXsWPMdJZvaiYznz1xUPZ
(private to the owner until shared).

`index.html` is the source of that page. It expects screenshots under
`shots/dark/*.png` and `shots/light/*.png` next to it; regenerate them with
`scripts/p7-mockups/` (see its README). Screenshots are not committed.

What's in it:

1. Every logged-in screen as it renders today (real components, mock data),
   in dark and light.
2. The proposed P7 restyle for Home, History, Messages, Profile, Venue chat,
   Members and Admin (the other organizer pages follow the Venue chat pattern).
3. Recommendations: what to change / keep, a WCAG contrast table, a
   touch-target table, and every remaining `TODO(P7)` literal.
4. A manual checklist for the founder's logged-in dark/light pass.
